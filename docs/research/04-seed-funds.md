## Practical order

1. Take the NSF Project Pitch assessment linked from the AR/VR topic page (F1) before writing a full proposal.
2. Keep the technical volume honest about phone LiDAR versus TLS (Table 1). NSF reviewers can read a brochure claim as the product spec.
3. Re-check NIST and DOE topic lists when their next NOFO opens. Ignore the July 2026 HUD date unless a new notice appears.

## Not found

No dedicated “construction digital twin seed fund” with an open 2026 call was found beyond the programs above. State economic-development matches and corporate constructor pilots were not surveyed.
