# Table 1 — Sensing modalities

Question this table answers: which sensor class can support residential **stud plumb QA** at about **0.12°** (derived from 1/4 inch in 10 feet; see [../tolerances.md](../tolerances.md)), i.e. a few millimeters of tip offset on an 8 foot stud.

**Short comparison.** High-end terrestrial laser scanners publish millimeter 3D accuracy and arcsecond-class angular specs at 10 m (vendor claims). Phone LiDAR publications found in this pass are **centimeter-class**. UWB and TI mmWave reference designs are **centimeter to decimeter** presence/ranging sensors, not edge metrology. XREAL Aura’s official page describes tracking cameras and a display, not a survey scanner. Photogrammetry accuracy is scene- and control-dependent; no stud-plumb spec was found. None of these rows is a measured OpenWall error budget.

Prior internal claim of **±3–10 mm RMSE at 1.5–2 m** for phone LiDAR: **unverified** this pass. Do not use it as a number.

## Stud-plumb reading of the table

| Need | What the sources support | What they do not support |
| --- | --- | --- |
| See a framed room quickly and train or demo a segmenter | Phone LiDAR / Polycam PLY, with centimeter-class error | A red/green call at 1/4 inch in 10 feet |
| Check a stud against ~0.12° | TLS-class instrument specs are in the right order of magnitude | A published “phone or radar meets 0.12° on 8 ft studs” result (none found) |
| Overlay the result in AR | Aura-class glasses as a display | Using Aura tracking cameras as the survey sensor |
| Detect that a person entered the room | UWB or mmWave reference designs | Measuring lumber |

Derived tip offsets are in [../tolerances.md](../tolerances.md). A full error stack (range noise + registration + segmentation + OBB axis + gravity) is **unknown** for every row.
